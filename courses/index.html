Track 01

Technician License Course

Complete beginner to ready-to-pass in four 2-hour sessions. No experience required, no Morse code, all ages welcome. Free exam session included.

What’s covered

  • FCC rules, privileges & operating basics
  • VHF/UHF practices, electronics, antennas & RF safety — all in plain language
  • Study alongside HamStudy.org; instructor tracks your progress between sessions
  • Dedicated exam-prep & practice-test session included
  • Free license exam at SPARC — walk in a beginner, walk out licensed
  • Scout and STEM groups welcome

Schedule options

Option A — Tue/Thu evenings Four weeknight sessions over two weeks
Option B — Sunday afternoons Two full Sunday sessions

Dates are set once a cohort fills. We’ll confirm your schedule by email before locking in a date.

Cost & location

  • Free to attend
  • Held at Balance Gaming, 6701 49th St N, Pinellas Park, FL 33781
  • Exam is free — the only cost is the FCC license application fee ($35, paid directly to the FCC after you pass)
