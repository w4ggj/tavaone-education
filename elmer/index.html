Elmer

A free tutor for people getting or upgrading a US amateur radio licence. No account, no sign-up, nothing collected. Works offline once loaded.

What Elmer knows

  • Licensing — the current NCVEC question pools, US privileges by class and band, and Part 97 of the FCC rules
  • Operating — standard band plans and calling frequencies, and Parks on the Air program rules and parks
  • Local — clubs in the Tampa Bay area, and where to look up repeaters and exam sessions

Everything here comes from those sources. Every frequency, power limit and pool answer is read from a table, not generated. Elmer does not know what's happening on the air right now, and it won't recommend gear or troubleshoot your rig.
