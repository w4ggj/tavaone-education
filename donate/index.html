Support the mission

Help build the room where the next generation gets on the air.

TavaOne Education pairs new and aspiring hams with experienced operators — the Elmer tradition, alive and hands-on. We’re building a real teaching station where beginners can sit down, key up, and make their first contact. Your gift puts them in the chair.

The problem we’re solving

Getting licensed is the easy part.

Staying on the air is where most newcomers quit — no gear, no mentor, no place to try. A dedicated teaching station removes every one of those barriers. It’s where curiosity becomes a lifelong operator.
