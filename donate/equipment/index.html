Equipment wishlist

Gear that turns our teaching station into a working classroom.

These are the pieces we need to put brand-new operators on the air. Gently-used gear in working condition is very welcome — a radio sitting idle in your shack can put a brand-new operator on the air. Prefer to give cash toward a specific item or a gift card to a ham retailer? That works too. Every donation receives a receipt.

Specific models shown are examples. Comparable gear is great.

Other options

Prefer not to shop for gear?

Gift cards

A gift card to DX Engineering, Ham Radio Outlet, or GigaParts lets us buy exactly what’s missing when we need it.

Cash toward a line item

Any amount toward a specific item above works. Just note the item when you give and we’ll apply it there. Every gift receives a receipt.

Software (free — listed for transparency, no donation needed): WSJT-X (FT8), GridTracker, and a logging program are all free and open-source. Donations go to hardware, not software.
