Pinellas STEM Radio Lab

A youth amateur-radio and STEM lab with permanently installed stations, operated by TavaOne Education and operating on the air as N4TAV. Kids learn electronics, licensing, and how to actually make contacts — then get on the radio themselves.

The Facility

A real station, not a demo table.

The lab is a dedicated built-out room — the same space that runs Balance Gaming's match-stream broadcasts — with HF and digital stations wired in. This isn't a folding table at a club meeting. It's a named STEM facility under a 501(c)(3), open to students, scouts, schools, and anyone who wants to actually learn.

📡

Installed stations

HF and digital modes wired in permanently. Show up and operate — no assembly required.

📻

On the air as N4TAV

An FCC-licensed club station. Every contact counts toward real operating history under a real callsign.

🏫

Run as education, not a club

TavaOne Education is a Florida 501(c)(3). The lab exists to teach, not to win contests. Students come first.

Programs

Get your license here.

Technician License Course

6 sessions / 12 hours Ages 13+ Exam on site

Four instruction sessions, a review session, then the exam — administered by a Volunteer Examiner team via SPARC. Cohorts run on weeknights or Sundays once a group fills. The same course is offered to students at the adjacent Kinney Karate dojo through a "Belt to Beacon" ladder (earn your belt, earn your license).
